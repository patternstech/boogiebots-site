The Experiences

Six robot and tech attractions you can book on their own or combine for a party nobody forgets.

The Headliner

BoogieBot Humanoid

Meet our Unitree G1 humanoid robot, the star of the party. It hits the dance floor, works the crowd with a stand-up comedy set and has real conversations with your guests about the topics they know and love.

  • Dance performances and dance-offs with guests
  • Stand-up comedy set tailored to your crowd
  • Mingles and chats with guests about their interests
  • Greetings, photo ops and birthday shout-outs
Book the Humanoid

Mixed Reality

VR Games at Your Place

We bring the VR arcade to you. Using Meta Quest headsets managed with SynthesisVR, we map your living room, backyard or venue so guests play mixed reality games in the space around them. Virtual action blends right into the real party.

  • Your home or backyard becomes the game arena
  • Mixed reality lets players see their surroundings
  • Multiplayer games for friends, families and teams
  • Our team sets up, runs and supervises every session
Book VR Games

Robot Art

Robot Sketch Artist

Our robotic arm picks up a pen and draws live in front of your guests. Guests watch each line appear and take home a one-of-a-kind keepsake.

  • Live sketches drawn by a robotic arm
  • Names, messages and custom designs
  • A take-home keepsake for guests
Book the Sketch Artist

Robot Maker

Robot 3D Printing

The same robotic arm swaps to a 3D printing head and builds objects layer by layer. It's a fascinating live demo and a fun way to make party souvenirs.

  • Live 3D printing performed by a robot arm
  • Small souvenirs and party favors
  • A hands-on look at how 3D printing works
Book 3D Printing

Robot Personalization

Robot Laser Engraving

With a laser engraving head, our robot arm personalizes party favors on the spot. Guests get their names, dates or designs etched into keepsakes they'll actually keep.

  • Custom names, dates, logos and designs
  • Personalized party favors made live
  • Run and supervised by our team
Book Laser Engraving

Roaming Photo Booth Robot

  • Moves around the venue to find guests
  • Captures candid and posed photos
  • A crowd favorite for all ages
